# First-user experiments

## 1. Direct seeding

Hypothesis: a consented research-interview follow-up reaches a small segment
with the stated evidence problem. Channel: selected users only. Batch and
capacity remain bounded. Stop if support capacity is exceeded.

## 2. First-five review

Hypothesis: the first evidence-backed decision is a candidate value moment, not
proof of repeat value. Stop new invitations at five users and review the value
observation, support load, and counter-metric before considering another loop.
