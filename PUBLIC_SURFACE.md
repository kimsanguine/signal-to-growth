# Public Surface Policy

This public repository contains the portable skills, contracts, runtime artifacts,
fixtures, and minimal reader guides required to use and verify Signal to Growth.

- Any `docs` or `.archive` directory at any depth is local/private and must not
  be added to Git.
- Internal planning, operational runbooks, evaluation notes, and handoffs are
  preserved outside this repository.
- Generated public runtime projections belong under `runtime/`, not `docs/`.
- This policy does not authorize external writes, provider setup, or deployment.
