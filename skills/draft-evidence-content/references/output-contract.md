# Output contract

## `content-brief.md`

State audience, question, intent, direct answer, evidence scope, limitations, CTA, and publication boundary.

## `claim-ledger.jsonl`

For each material claim include:

- claim ID and exact wording;
- claim state;
- evidence IDs or source URLs;
- source date;
- public eligibility;
- owner and approval;
- refresh date when time-sensitive.

## `draft.md`

Use answer-first structure. Keep citations near supported claims and disclose limitations where they affect the decision.

## `review-checklist.md`

Cover accuracy, evidence references, privacy, brand, legal, commercial claim, CTA, and final publication approval.

## Completion gate

No material claim is unsupported, no restricted evidence is exposed, and publication has not occurred.
