# Output contract

## `interview-guide.md`

Include:

- consent opening;
- recent-event anchor;
- first thought;
- passive and active looking;
- decision and trade-offs;
- commitment or purchase;
- first use and value;
- Push, Pull, Anxiety, and Habit probes;
- closing timeline check.

## `timeline-notes.md`

Keep participant statements, interviewer observations, uncertainty, and interpretation visually separate. Record approximate time when exact time is unknown.

## `follow-up-questions.md`

List unresolved timeline gaps and the evidence each follow-up should clarify. Do not write expected answers.

## Completion gate

The guide is ready when it centers on a real past event, passes question lint review, and consent handling is explicit.
