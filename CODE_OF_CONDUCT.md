# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want Signal to Growth to be a useful, evidence-respecting project where
people can participate without harassment or intimidation.

Examples of expected behavior:

- discuss ideas and evidence, not personal characteristics;
- give specific, constructive feedback;
- disclose uncertainty and correct mistakes;
- protect customer data, credentials, and private account details;
- respect consent, attribution, and human approval boundaries.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
deliberate exposure of private information, impersonation, spam, and repeated
disruption of project work.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release
work, and project-related public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not post sensitive incident details publicly. Report security or privacy
issues through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting for this repository.
For other conduct concerns, use GitHub's reporting and blocking tools and, when
safe, contact the repository maintainer through a non-public channel.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or report
abuse to the hosting platform. Enforcement decisions should minimize further
exposure of affected people and private data.
